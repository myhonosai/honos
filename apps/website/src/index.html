faq

Questions owners ask.

Do I pay for AI messages?

No. The AI team is included in your subscription. There are no per-message charges and chatting with HON never uses credits.

Does HON take a cut of my tuition?

No. You connect your own Stripe or Square account and HON takes no fee on your tuition. Online payments from families through that account are coming soon; today you record cash, check and card-terminal payments in HON. Your processor's own fees still apply.

Can I move my students over?

Yes. Import students and guardians from a CSV file exported from your current system.

What languages does HON OS support?

English, Korean and Spanish. Each person on your team picks their own language and region.

What happens after the free trial?

Nothing is charged automatically. When your 14 days are up, choose monthly or yearly to keep going.
