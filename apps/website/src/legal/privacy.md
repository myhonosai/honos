This Privacy Policy explains how [[Legal entity name, for example HON OS Labs LLC]] ("HON", "we", "us") handles personal information in HON OS, the HON Family parent app, HON HQ, the HON Shopify app and this website (together, the "Services").

HON OS is software that martial arts schools use to run their business. Most of the personal information in HON OS is about a school's own students, families, staff and leads, and the school decides what to collect and how to use it. For that information the school is in charge and HON works on the school's behalf, under the data processing terms in our [Terms of Service](/terms/#data-processing-terms-for-schools). If you are a student, parent or lead of a school, please also read that school's own privacy notice, and contact the school first with questions about your information.

## Who this policy covers

- **Schools and their staff**: owners, managers, instructors and front desk staff who have a HON OS account.
- **Students, parents and guardians, and leads** whose information a school keeps in HON OS, including parents who use the HON Family app.
- **Merchants** who connect a Shopify store to HON OS through the HON Shopify app.
- **Visitors** to this website.

## Information we collect

### Information a school puts into HON OS

Depending on the features the school uses, this can include:

- **Contact and family details**: names, email addresses, phone numbers, mailing addresses, birth dates, emergency contacts, and how family members are related.
- **Training records**: programs, memberships, class bookings, attendance and check-ins, belt ranks and testing results, achievements, notes staff write, and signed waivers and agreements.
- **Photos**: profile photos and pictures a school chooses to upload.
- **Messages**: texts, emails and in-app messages between the school and its students, families and leads, with delivery records and opt-out (STOP) and help (HELP) requests.
- **Billing records**: plans, invoices, tuition amounts, payment status and the last digits and brand of a saved card. Card numbers are entered directly with the payment provider and never stored by HON.
- **Leads and marketing**: enquiries from website forms, missed calls, and Facebook and Instagram lead forms, with how each lead found the school.
- **Staff records**: staff accounts, roles and permissions, shifts and the actions staff take in HON OS.

### Information about school owners and staff

When you create or use a HON OS account we collect your name, email address, phone number, school name and legal name, time zone and school address, your sign-in details, your language and display preferences, and the actions you take in HON OS (kept in an audit log so the school can see who did what).

### Information about HON OS subscriptions

When a school subscribes to HON OS, our payment provider (Stripe) collects the payment card and billing details. We receive the subscription status, plan, invoices, amounts and the last digits and brand of the card, never the full card number.

### Information from connected services

When a school chooses to connect another service, HON receives only what that connection needs:

- **Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google and Zernio**: the school's page or account names and identifiers, leads submitted through the school's lead forms, and the reporting and posting details the school's marketing features use. Access tokens are stored encrypted.
- **Square and Stripe for tuition**: when a school takes tuition payments through its own Square or Stripe account, we receive the payment and payout records needed to show them in HON OS.
- **Shopify**: see "The HON Shopify app" below.

### Face check-in (only when a school turns it on)

Face check-in is off unless a school turns it on, and it is only available in places where HON has made it available. Before any face data is created, the school must collect written consent: from a parent or guardian for any student under 18 (or with no birth date on file), and from adult students themselves. HON then creates a face template from the student's profile photo, used only to suggest a match at that one school, and a staff member confirms every match before attendance is recorded. The camera picture is never stored: it is compared and then discarded. Anyone can refuse or withdraw consent at any time and still check in another way, and refusing never affects classes or membership. Face templates are never sold, never shared and never used for anything other than check-in at that school. See "How long we keep information" for when templates are deleted.

### Website visitors

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising trackers and has no forms. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) for security and reliability.

### Information collected automatically in the apps

HON OS and the HON Family app use cookies or secure app storage to keep you signed in and to remember your language and display choices. We keep technical logs (such as IP address, device and browser type, and error reports) to run, secure and fix the Services.

## How we use information

We use personal information to:

- provide the Services the school asked for, such as managing students and families, classes, attendance, belts, billing, messages and marketing;
- run the AI features the school turns on (see "AI features");
- keep the Services secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and keep audit records of who did what;
- send service messages to school owners and staff, such as account, billing and security notices;
- provide support when a school asks for help;
- improve the Services using information that no longer identifies anyone; and
- meet legal obligations, such as tax records and texting rules.

We use the information a school puts into HON OS only to provide the Services to that school and as the school instructs. **We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.** We do not use student or family information to advertise to students or families.

## AI features

HON OS includes AI assistants that help a school draft messages, summarize records, suggest follow-ups, create marketing images and videos, and similar tasks. When a school uses these features, the information needed for the task is sent to the AI service provider that performs it. HON requires those providers not to use school information to train their models. **HON does not use a school's student, family or staff information to train AI models.** Actions that send messages, spend money or change important records go through approval rules the school controls, and staff should review AI output before relying on it.

## The HON Shopify app

When a merchant connects a Shopify store to HON OS, HON asks Shopify for read-only access to products, inventory and orders. HON copies:

- products and variants (title, status, SKU, barcode, price, unit cost and stock);
- orders from the last 60 days and new orders after that: order number, dates, status, sales channel, currency, subtotal, discounts, tax and total; and
- order line items: product title, SKU, quantity and unit price.

**HON stores no Shopify customer names, emails, phone numbers or addresses.** We use this information only to show the merchant their catalog, stock, sales and estimated margins inside HON OS. HON never writes to the store. Access tokens are encrypted, and the database never sees them in readable form.

When a merchant uninstalls the HON app or disconnects the store, HON destroys its access tokens at once. When Shopify sends its shop deletion request (48 hours after an uninstall), HON erases that store's products, orders and order lines and keeps only a record that the store was connected and erased. Because HON holds no Shopify customer data, customer data requests and customer deletion requests from Shopify have nothing to return or erase, and we record that we checked.

## Children's privacy

HON OS is used by schools that teach children, so a school may keep information about students under 13. That information is provided by the school or by a parent or guardian, not collected directly from children by HON. Schools agree in our Terms to obtain any parental consent the law requires, including under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), before adding a child's information or turning on features such as face check-in. Children do not get their own HON OS or HON Family accounts; the HON Family app is for parents, guardians and adult students. A parent or guardian can ask the school, or us, to review or delete their child's information and to stop further collection; we will work with the school to do that. If we learn that we have collected a child's information directly without the required consent, we will delete it.

## How we share information

We share personal information only:

- **With the school**: information in a school's account is available to that school's authorized staff, according to the roles the school sets. Families see their own information in the HON Family app.
- **With service providers** that host, secure and run the Services for us, under contracts that limit their use of the information to providing their service (see "Service providers" below).
- **With services the school connects**, when the school asks us to, such as sending a text, posting to the school's social accounts or taking a payment.
- **For legal reasons**, when required by law or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety and security of HON, schools, families or the public.
- **In a business transfer**, such as a merger or sale of HON, where the information stays subject to this policy.

## Service providers

We use the following kinds of providers. Each only receives the information it needs, and some are used only when a school turns on the feature that needs them. [[Confirm this list against signed provider agreements before publishing.]]

- **Hosting and database**: Render (application hosting) and Supabase (database and sign-in), in the United States.
- **Payments**: Stripe (HON OS subscriptions, and tuition for schools that use Stripe) and Square (tuition for schools that use Square).
- **Texting and calls**: Twilio (text messages and missed-call text-back).
- **Email**: Twilio SendGrid (service and school emails).
- **AI**: AI model providers such as Anthropic and OpenAI, and media generation providers for marketing images, video and voice, used only for AI features.
- **Marketing connections**: Meta, Google and Zernio, used only when a school connects them.
- **Commerce**: Shopify, used only when a merchant connects a store.

## Texting

Schools use HON OS to text their students, families and leads, and must have the person's permission to do so. Anyone can reply **STOP** to stop texts from that school and **HELP** for help. HON records opt-outs and stops texts to that number from that school until the person opts back in. Message and data rates may apply. We do not share mobile numbers or texting consent with third parties for their own marketing.

## How long we keep information

- **School account data** is kept while the school's subscription is active. After a school closes its account, we keep it for [[number]] days so the school can export it, then delete or de-identify it, except where we must keep records longer (for example invoices and tax records, which we keep for as long as tax law requires).
- **Face templates** are deleted at the earliest of: consent is withdrawn; the profile photo is removed or replaced; the school turns face check-in off; 30 days after the student is no longer active; or 12 months without a face check-in. We keep only a record of who, when and why, with no face data.
- **Shopify data** is erased as described in "The HON Shopify app".
- **Opt-out (STOP) records** are kept so we keep honoring them.
- **Audit logs and security logs** are kept for [[number]] months to keep the Services secure and show who did what.
- **Backups** are overwritten on a rolling schedule of about [[number]] days.

## Security

We protect information with encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest, encrypted storage of access tokens and other secrets, strict separation between schools enforced in the database, role-based permissions for staff, audit logs of important actions, and limited access by HON personnel. No system is perfectly secure; if a security incident affects your information, we will notify the school and, where the law requires, the people affected and the authorities.

## Your choices and rights

- **Students, parents and leads**: contact your school to see, correct, export or delete your information or your child's information. If you contact us, we will pass your request to the school and help the school respond.
- **School owners and staff**: you can see and update your account information in HON OS, and contact us to export or delete it.
- **Texts**: reply STOP to any text to opt out.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to access, correct or delete it, to get a copy, and to opt out of its sale or sharing (we do not sell or share it). You will not be treated differently for using these rights. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and you can use an authorized agent.

### California residents

California law gives residents the rights described above. In the last 12 months we have collected the categories of information listed in "Information we collect" (identifiers, contact details, commercial and billing records, internet activity in our logs, and, only where a school turns on face check-in with consent, biometric information), for the purposes in "How we use information". We have not sold or shared personal information, and we do not knowingly sell or share the information of anyone under 16. We use sensitive personal information only to provide the Services.

## Where information is stored

HON OS is offered to schools in the United States, and information is stored and processed in the United States.
