# Product vision, user journeys, plan capabilities and presentation

## Positioning

**HON OS — The AI-powered operating system built to run and grow martial arts schools.**

The primary buyer is an independent martial arts school owner (Taekwondo, BJJ and related disciplines), later multi-location operators. HON was conceived by an operating school owner; do not position the founder as a marketing consultant or promise expert consultations as referral prizes. Sell meaningful software and measurable operating outcomes, not an AI chatbot demo.

HON differentiates through **one coherent lifecycle** of school operations, real Meta/Google marketing where connected/authorized, attributed acquisition-to-enrollment outcomes, and delegated school AI work within approvals. Established competitors have portions of these features; validate any comparative claim before publishing.

## Canonical school lifecycle

Lead (from manual, web, Meta or Google where connected) -> follow-up -> trial -> attended trial -> enrollment -> signed/authorized membership -> payment -> class schedule -> attendance -> curriculum/progression -> testing -> family engagement -> retention/renewal -> reporting.

First-win journey: public offer -> verified signup -> school onboarding -> program/class setup -> create/import prospect -> schedule trial -> issue truthful enrollment/membership state -> authorized billing -> reports. Do not imply payment has begun on an unsigned/unfinished registration or that a DRAFT receipt establishes agreement or collection. Payment start and contractual consent timing require explicit school policy and approved implementation.

Core is a full **conventional operating platform** rather than a limited teaser CRM. Operational screens remain usable if AI, voice or external marketing providers are unavailable.

## Presentation

Three equivalent functional presentation modes: **Big-Button / Phone**, **Modern HON**, **Classic CRM**. They must operate on the same records, guards, permissions and feature tiers; presentation selection does not grant an entitlement. Realistic/Anime/Chibi AI styles similarly affect presentation only. The compact preference selector belongs directly under the school/location dropdown in the applicable school view; public pricing does NOT belong in the school's command top bar.

The brand is AI-first but must be trustworthy: real results, real task states, clear drafts versus active campaigns, paid versus pending invoices, no fictitious charts, seats, awards, trials, conversion or agent work. Support human-friendly navigation rather than requiring routine manual UUID entry.

## HON HQ

HQ is a **separate SaaS operator plane**, not another school account: plans/entitlements, schools, access grants, AI provider credit economics, billing/refunds/support, promotion campaigns, verified referral rewards/contests, provider connection administration, legitimate asset migration controls and governed HQ AI executives. Routine operation after launch must be in HON HQ, with no coding assistant required.

Cross-account asset transfer needs explicit ownership, scopes, operator authority, consent/audit and approved transfer list. Never use it to move private student records, secrets, tokens or card details through a generic transfer command.
