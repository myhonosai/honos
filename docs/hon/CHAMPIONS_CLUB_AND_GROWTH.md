# HON Champions Club, founder launch and growth plan

**Launch-critical BUSINESS REQUIREMENT, not evidence of current software implementation.**

## Day-one permanent referral program

Every subscribing eligible school should see **HON Champions Club** from Day 1 of commercial launch: personal referral URL, share text/QR code, invitation landing page, $100 welcome-credit explanation, status, progress bar, earned rewards, permanent all-time + monthly standings, privacy options, leaderboard and rewards history. Never invent live participant rankings or seed fictional winners as facts.

A newly referred eligible school gets **$100 HON welcome credit**, not a default cash/Visa award. Display the **30-day HON plan money-back guarantee**, Core and Complete choices, and available founder terms on an attributed landing page. Attribution survives signup and payment via secure server-issued referral identity; avoid fraud/self-referrals/duplicates and never let a URL parameter grant financial entitlement by itself.

Referrer milestone **owner direction** (qualified, verified referred schools):
- **Referrals 1–4:** $100 HON credit to referrer **per qualified referral**, and $100 welcome credit to each new school.
- **5 referrals:** referring school reaches **6 months HON free**, including its ordinary monthly premium tier credits; new school gets $100.
- **10 referrals:** referring school reaches **12 months HON free in total**, including (not on top of) six already earned at 5; new school gets $100.
- **20 referrals:** **owner-selectable physical/experience GRAND PRIZE**, e.g. consumer electronics, gaming system, iPad/TV, Disneyland/Universal-type family amusement tickets, weekend experience; target example maximum prize procurement budget **$1,000**, *not finalized or published*; new school gets $100.
- **30 referrals:** **lifetime HON membership** at the referrer's eligible plan, with that plan's included monthly premium AI credit allowance for the life of the covered service; new school gets $100.

For counts 6–9, 11–19 and 21–29, continuing $100 rewards were discussed but **final terms are open**. Owner may instead choose subscription, eligible AI credits, select merchandise or a digital Visa prepaid gift card. Gift cards have tax/reporting and material-connection disclosure considerations; never call a Visa prize tax-free. For pre-existing paid lifetime members who cannot redeem free months or another lifetime service, specify a **distinct approved substitute** (gift card/merchandise) before customer-facing activation.

Important pending decisions: define qualification timing (after 30-day refund eligibility; 60 days/two paid invoices discussed but not approved), attribution window, cancellations/clawbacks, whether limits apply by company/beneficial owner, rule for founder/annual subscribers, final prize taxes/delivery and reward-value cap. Always require explicit activation/award approval where financially consequential.

**Reward milestones are cumulative, not stacked duplicate long free periods.** Every awarded free month preserves the subscriber's included premium credits; no secret downgraded allowance.

## Leaderboard — Day 1, real data only

Real verified referrals drive both the permanent rankings and progress. Show all-time, monthly, private personal rank and a special contest leaderboard only when a contest is active. Participation/display-name consent is opt-in; avoid publishing nonconsenting school names or private customer data. Defend against gaming, cancellations, repeated qualifying payments and retroactive attribution.

## HQ contest engine — owner controls, no Manus needed

**HON HQ owns all configuration**, while Core and Complete school dashboards provide opt-in participation. Permanent rewards remain separate and always-on. HQ admins can create/draft, configure dates, prizes, budgets, eligibility, qualification rules, review legal terms, **schedule, launch, pause, resume, end, freeze results, review winners and approve fulfillment**. Cannot unilaterally disadvantage entrants by editing live rules. Keep versioned terms and full staff/action audit. Authorized AI executive can *propose* a contest but cannot publish, spend or award unapproved prizes.

During a qualified active contest, one verified referred-school outcome may count toward **both** permanent milestone progress and the contest competition if rules allow; two reward liabilities are recorded distinctly, never double disbursed. Day-one contest builder capability exists even when no special contest is active.

Potential OPTIONAL quarterly/90-day contest example: 1st a curated $1,000 electronics/family experience prize, 2nd $500 and 3rd $250 budget; **illustrative**, not active or funded. No purchase-required random sweepstakes without appropriate approved contest rules and compliance review.

## Founder commercial-launch campaign

**Founding 50:** 20 original paid lifetime offers and 30 monthly founders, with prices in `OWNER_COMMERCIAL_POLICY.md`. Black-Friday-season framing may start early November rather than on one day; actual launch requires readiness and owner approval. Retain a 30-day refundable purchase window with sufficient reserve; never assume conversion or published availability before checkout/accounting exists.

## Acquisition order — association partnerships LAST

1. Founder-owned school demonstrations and verified owner-operated case study; demonstrate HON Core and Complete solving actual school work.
2. Real Founding 50 scarcity campaign, self-serve/public signup with honest statuses, opt-in founder-led demos, 30-day full eligible-plan refund.
3. **HON Champions Club referrals**, live leaderboard and occasional HQ-started referral championship. Prize format is fun owner/family experiences, NOT a marketing-coaching package.
4. Helpful educational content, authentic founder story, school owner communities, product-led free utilities (e.g. ad creator/calculator), social proof and case studies; controlled small paid ads after measuring payback.
5. Independent ambassadors, coaching circles, local operator networks and joint education after direct proof. **Associations/tournaments/national association partnerships only after measured results and testimonials.**

Operating objective: **1,000 active paying schools by month 12**; **350 by month 6** is an ambitious planning checkpoint, not evidence or guarantee. Measure conversion from view -> eligible lead -> demo/trial -> 30-day retained paying school -> retained/referring customer; track actual CAC, contribution margin, refund rate, churn, referrals per customer, prize liability and return of approved advertising spend. Account for founders and lifetime recurring provider cost.

## Legal, safety and owner credibility

Make platform product claims verifiable. Incentivized endorsements should disclose material relationships where required. Physical prizes and Visa prepaid cards can have tax/reporting implications. No buyer should need to be a marketing expert to use HON; HON is created by an experienced martial-arts operator, not sold as a consultant's personal marketing service.
