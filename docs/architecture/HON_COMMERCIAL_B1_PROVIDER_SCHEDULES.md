## Implemented boundaries and routes

`POST /v1/schools/:schoolId/hon-os-subscription-generations` requires a live signed-session exact-school human with `billing.hon.purchase`, the existing same-origin human-command guard, `Idempotency-Key`, and `x-hon-human-command: saas-subscription-generation-record`. Closed body: `{acceptanceId, acceptanceDigest, confirmation:"RECORD_SUBSCRIPTION_GENERATION_ONLY_NO_BILLING"}`. No amount, interval, phase, provider-account selection, founder evidence, eligibility, subscription, payment or access fields are accepted. This records an inert intent, not consent to process a payment.

`GET /v1/schools/:schoolId/hon-os-subscription-generations/:generationId` independently reauthorizes that exact purchaser scope. `GET /v1/hq/saas/subscription-generations/:generationId` independently requires HQ owner/billing-operator authority. Reads are private/no-store, closed and read-only. There is no provider-evidence write endpoint. All three calls use the fixed runtime operation registry and the canonical SaaS service error mapping.

One generation per acceptance is protected by a database unique constraint; different idempotency keys cannot duplicate it. A later distinct customer acceptance may have its own inert intent. B2 must independently enforce the one-live-subscription/per-organization lifecycle when selecting a generation; B1 never attaches a generation to an existing subscription or changes historical rights. New intents require the accepted initial offer still on sale, so emergency stop/new publication cannot be bypassed. Previously completed exact-request receipts remain historical and replayable after a stop; they grant no ability to purchase.

Error outcomes: invalid closed input 400; missing/foreign acceptance 404 after independent school authorization; forbidden/revoked actor 403; stale acceptance digest or duplicate-generation conflict 409; incomplete evidence 422; in-flight/timeout `SAAS_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` 409 with explicit exact-request retry; lost database result 503 without an invented success. No automatic resubmission occurs. References and retries must remain on the server/in caller memory, never public URLs or local browser storage.

## Provider parity and paid-period transition design

The immutable generation includes every accepted A2 term, offer snapshot and disclosure, plus a server-owned generation UUID. `subscriptionScheduleRequirements` constructs the provider request from that record. The provider-independent adapter has no real implementation/production registration in B1. `UnconfiguredSubscriptionScheduleProvider` refuses before I/O; the deterministic fake is under `apps/api/test/fixtures` only. A fake observation is closed, TEST-only, cannot claim provider verification and cannot be stored as a binding. Parity checks bind exact identities, amounts, intervals, introductory count, counting rule, terms/disclosure and no automatic collection. Extra/mismatching/unknown/calendar-only observations produce REVIEW_REQUIRED. Even PARITY_VERIFIED_TEST_ONLY returns billing/checkout false and is not commercial evidence.

If the future processor cannot natively count verified paid periods, B2 must keep automatic calendar transition/collection off and use a separately reviewed release gate. Before each invoice/phase release it must lock the generation and provider binding in canonical order, reauthorize execution, reconcile distinct authenticated provider invoices for the exact account/subscription and covered period, and stop on pending/failed/unknown/reversed/disputed evidence. Only qualifying verified paid periods can advance the accepted introductory count. At 12 monthly or one annual qualifying period, the release amount must equal the fixed accepted renewal amount and disclosure. A missing gate, uncertain provider state, unresolved reversal policy, mismatched account/amount/interval or unverified binding leaves release/checkout unavailable. No date-only phase or webhook count may substitute. This is an implementation requirement for B2, not an implemented invoice-release engine.

The exported `SaasPaidPeriodEvidenceCandidate` and `SaasRenewalTransitionReview` are interfaces only, with no persistence route/reducer/financial execution. B2 must add unique authenticated invoice references scoped by provider/mode/account, distinguish event deduplication from covered-period uniqueness, preserve receipt/digest/observed/verified timestamps, retain original invoices and linked reversals, and reconcile out-of-order events. A receipt alone cannot mark a period paid or a subscription active. Refund/dispute counting and cancellation/promotion reuse are REVIEW_REQUIRED owner decisions; B1 supplies no invented policy or automatic resolution.
