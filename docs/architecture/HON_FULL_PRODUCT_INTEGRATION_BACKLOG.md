## B-OPS-4 — End-to-end commercial rehearsal design (not executable yet)

This scenario list is the future R1 operational proof after the required S1–S8 and T contract slices, identity path, ledger attestation, and provider/test-environment approvals exist. It is neither a test authorization nor a customer workflow today.

1. **Public signup / school identity:** create or invite one school through the approved public-identity and D7 caller, prove exact organization/school ownership and deny cross-school access.
2. **Fourteen-day trial:** start a policy-approved, no-automatic-billing trial; record immutable campaign/consent/eligibility evidence; prove that a trial neither reserves a Founder allocation nor activates paid access by implication.
3. **Founder purchase:** execute one separately human-confirmed, test-only founder purchase only after eligibility, allocation, schedule, price-acceptance, provider evidence, and authorization gates agree. Prove exactly one allocation, ledger outcome, audit record, and outbox effect on replay.
4. **Renewal:** reconcile a scheduled renewal using the original provider/idempotency identity; distinguish provider-observed, locally recorded, unknown, and declined states without duplicate charge, credit, or entitlement changes.
5. **Failed payment:** route failure to the owner-approved dunning/hold/reconciliation state. The system must not represent payment as successful or silently delete the original evidence.
6. **Cancellation:** bind a qualified human decision to the precise subscription/schedule and policy version; preserve historical invoice, entitlement, audit, and revenue-recognition evidence.
7. **Refund:** handle only through the distinct, human-authorized refund/adjustment policy, provider reconciliation, and immutable financial evidence path. It must never be inferred from cancellation or an AI request.

Each rehearsal step must demonstrate UI → protected API → controller/guard → domain service → PostgreSQL/RLS → audit/idempotency/outbox → reconciliation/recovery. The companion B-OPS-3 backup/restore plus deploy/rollback runbook cannot be finalized until the owner selects the non-production hosting and recovery environment; it must prove backup recovery before any production deployment recommendation.
