Source baseline: `121653ee81d85a3ff893a209e635db2efaefff08` (A1 #74 and D11-A #72 merged). Migration **0188** follows 0187; 0179/0180 remain reserved. All previous migrations are unchanged. ## Data model

`saas_price_schedules` is append-only per exact published offer, with an expected version on each new record. It binds the offer/set ID, set version and revision, exact existing accepted-offer snapshot and digest, renewal-offer identity, initial/renewal USD minor amounts, interval, introductory paid-period count, period-count rule, immutable disclosure/version/language, refund/cancellation references, recording human, reason and canonical request identity. A content digest includes the complete versioned schedule. A new version supersedes a schedule for NEW acknowledgements; it never edits historical evidence.

`saas_price_acceptances` binds that exact immutable schedule to the verified organization and exact school, accepting live human, server timestamp, original idempotency key and explicit confirmation. A school-bound digest covers organization, school, schedule ID and schedule digest. The exact disclosure is copied into the acceptance record. The database unique key `(organization_id, school_id, schedule_id)` prevents a second staff member creating a duplicate acceptance. Same-key retries return the original receipt; changed requests are conflicts. An acknowledgement is not a charge, unrelated legal consent, signature, agreement, subscription, verified payment or software activation.

Both tables have forced RLS with no runtime table/column privileges, immutable update/delete/truncate guards, INSERT fact/evidence checks and deferred live-authority/audit/outbox verification. No rows or policies are seeded. Every command completes its record, canonical request, audit and inert `executionAllowed:false` outbox together, or rolls all of them back.

## Prices, references and disclosure

The HQ owner records a schedule by selecting an initial offer and the appropriate regular renewal offer **from the same published set**, supplying expected set revision/schedule version, fixed disclosure version, explicit recording confirmation and reason. No amount, discount or eligibility is accepted in the school command. The initial amount comes from the selected offer; the renewal amount from the explicitly bound regular monthly/annual offer; the monthly comparison reference must be the single unambiguous regular monthly offer in that set. Regular arrangements bind themselves. Unsupported lifetime, price-lock, nonrenewing annual, cross-set or interval combinations fail closed instead of being silently reinterpreted. A new schedule requires `priceLock=NONE`; existing price-locked customer agreements are not rewritten into it.

Approved launch direction (not a seeded/published configuration):

| Arrangement | Initial USD minor | Introductory qualifying paid periods | Renewal USD minor |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Founder monthly | 19920 | 12 monthly periods | 24900 |
| Founder annual | 149400 | 1 annual period | 209700 |
| Regular monthly | 24900 | 0 | 24900 |
| Regular annual | 209700 | 0 | 209700 |

Annual reference is **24900 × 12 = 298800**. Founder annual is 50% below that reference, never 50% below 209700. Standard annual **209700** is explicit, not a rounded 30% calculation. HQ may deliberately record later published configurations without rewriting prior acceptances. A1 tests retain exact percentage/rounding checks; A2 never accepts percentages or discount stacks.

The fixed English disclosure is generated by the database from exact cents. Founder examples:

- `$199.20 per month for the first 12 qualifying paid monthly periods. Thereafter, $249.00 per month until canceled under the accepted terms.`
- `$1,494.00 for the first annual subscription period. Renews at $2,097.00 annually unless canceled under the accepted terms.`

The display version is `HON_OS_PRICING_EN_V1`; Korean/Spanish legal/financial disclosures require separately reviewed versions later, not an automatic translation. Refund references include the accepted window; cancellation references include the accepted end-of-paid-period rule. Actual legal documents and consent to unrelated terms remain separate.

## Closed API contracts

All routes are under `/v1`, use the production signed-session identity guard, private/no-store reads and existing direct-human/same-origin command guard. Commands require JSON, `Idempotency-Key`, exact `x-hon-human-command`, and `Sec-Fetch-Site/Mode: same-origin`, `Sec-Fetch-Dest: empty`. No actor, tenant authority, payment or entitlement claims are accepted from the body.

| Route | Authority and meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `POST /hq/saas/price-schedules` | Live HQ owner only; `saas-price-schedule-record`; records an immutable schedule, not offer publication |
| `GET /hq/saas/price-schedules/:scheduleId` | HQ owner or billing operator; exact historical schedule |
| `GET /schools/:schoolId/hon-os-price-acceptances/preview/:scheduleId` | Exact-school `billing.hon.purchase`; read-only financial disclosure and acknowledgement blockers |
| `POST /schools/:schoolId/hon-os-price-acceptances` | Exact-school purchaser; `saas-price-schedule-accept`; digest-bound explicit acknowledgement |
| `GET /schools/:schoolId/hon-os-price-acceptances/:acceptanceId` | Independently reauthorized exact school/organization; historical receipt |

Record body: `{offerId, renewalOfferId, expectedOfferSetRevision, expectedVersion, disclosureVersion, confirmation:"RECORD_IMMUTABLE_PRICING_SCHEDULE_ONLY", reason}`.

Acceptance body: `{scheduleId, scheduleDigest, acceptanceDigest, confirmation:"ACCEPT_INITIAL_AND_SCHEDULED_RENEWAL_PRICES"}`. Values are taken from a fresh server preview; dates, amounts, duration, percentages, discounts, allocation claims and provider fields are forbidden.

Preview separately reports `canAcknowledge`, blockers, `purchaseAvailable:false`, `paymentAuthorized:false`, `accessGranted:false`. Suspended entitlements do not prevent independently authorized billing disclosure/recovery; reading/acknowledging grants no school operation or benefit. Receipt remains `NOT_AUTHORIZED` payment, `NOT_CREATED` subscription, unavailable Commercial B provider binding and no paid-period evidence. Unknown network outcome is not success: explicitly retry the unchanged request/key; never automatically resend or generate another key for an uncertain action.

New commands reject stale set/schedule revisions, digest mismatches, stopped/retired/unpublished/expired offers and unsupported terms. Authorization is checked before and after lock waits, on historical replay, and at commit. Historical replay intentionally returns its original evidence even after HQ publishes new prices or stops sales; it grants no purchasing right.

## Locking and concurrency

Read committed only. Both commands take the existing IAM table-share order (users, principals, role grants, family grants), protect HQ grants and role/permission/school/organization inputs, then claim the canonical idempotency key (existing nonblocking request mutex), acquire `saas.offer_sets`, then share-lock offer sets and sales stops. Live authority is rechecked after waiting. This serializes schedule revisions, new acceptances, publication and stop-sales. No inventory, organization advisory, entitlement, checkout, subscription, payment or trial lock is subsequently acquired. Existing checkout takes inventory → organization → offer table-share and never waits for A2 after that point; existing stop-sales takes offer-set mutex → inventory. A2 adds no reverse edge to either path. Locks are held to transaction end; deferred triggers also recheck clock-based grant/offer validity at commit.

Concurrent different-key acknowledgements have one winner; the loser creates no committed record, claim, audit or outbox. Same-key in-flight calls receive the existing recoverable `REQUEST_IN_PROGRESS`, with explicit exact-request retry after settlement. Competing HQ versions use expected revision/version; future/snapshot transaction isolation is refused rather than using stale authorization snapshots.

## Commercial B and C boundaries

**Commercial B is not implemented.** A BEFORE INSERT guard blocks the old checkout path for every schedule-bound offer with `SAAS_PRICE_PROVIDER_BINDING_REQUIRED`. Otherwise that path could ignore the new renewal commitment and keep charging its single accepted price. Existing checkouts/subscriptions/invoices/lifetime rights are untouched, and unbound historical payment proofs still use their original path. B must replace this deliberate refusal only in a separately reviewed schedule-aware purchasing generation.

B must bind the independently authorized acceptance ID/digest, exact existing accepted-offer lineage, verified school/organization, initial and fixed renewal amounts, exact customer disclosure/terms and provider-mode/account/schedule evidence. A provider billing schedule must be verified to honor all accepted phases BEFORE a payable session can be offered. Failed/unknown provider creation stays unavailable/review; it is never assumed successful. Never use legacy `acceptedOffer.recurringBilling` or a single `priceMinor` as a substitute for the new schedule.

Future provider binding, invoice evidence, successful paid-period evidence, failed/unknown attempts, renewal phase and paid-entitlement activation are separate contracts. Count distinct authoritative covered/verified paid billing periods, not elapsed calendar months, redirects, previews, webhooks without verification or duplicate invoices. B needs amount/phase parity, unique period/provider references, atomic evidence, no promotion advancement on failure, duplicate/out-of-order and recovery proofs, refund/dispute handling and preserved accepted rights. No function in A2 creates that evidence or a provider schedule.

**Commercial C is not implemented.** Founder preview shows `FOUNDER_ALLOCATION_CONTRACT_UNAVAILABLE`; all founder acceptance is refused, and the allocation field is constrained NULL. No synthetic allocation or eligibility claim can bypass it. C must provide independently authorized durable organization allocation/promotion-history evidence for the single global 50-paying-organization pool, bounded holds, anti-reuse and paid-review handling, then add a reviewed FK/binding/enforcement migration. A trial preview/acceptance cannot allocate a founder slot. Availability-only versus trial reservation remains an owner decision; no slot promise is made.

D11-A remains INACTIVE; publication is governance metadata, not trial runtime. Future conversion preserves the same organization/school and work history, but A2 creates no trial, invitation, enrollment, consent, entitlement or promotional funding.
