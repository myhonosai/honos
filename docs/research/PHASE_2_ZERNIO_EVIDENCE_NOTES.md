# Phase 2 Zernio Evidence Notes

Research was limited to the official Zernio documentation URLs supplied in `PHASE_2_ZERNIO_SOURCES.md` and the supplied HON readiness assessment. This is feasibility evidence only; it is not authorization to connect an account, register a webhook, perform OAuth, retrieve leads, or activate an ad/form.

## Webhook and lead-event contract

- Zernio documents `lead.received` for a Meta Lead Gen (Instant) form submission. It includes a stable Zernio event `id`, Meta `leadgenId`, `formId`, optional `formName`, optional ad/ad-set/campaign IDs, flattened `fields`, `isOrganic`, Meta `createdAt`, and a Facebook account object. The account object has the Zernio connection ID (`accountId`/`id`) and `profileId`; its platform is `facebook`. The event payload does not expose a Page ID. `formName` can be null and `adId` is null for organic/test leads. Multiple-choice answers are option keys rather than display labels. Source: <https://docs.zernio.com/webhooks/ads>.
- Zernio says to deduplicate leads by `lead.leadgenId` or event `id`. The generic webhook contract identifies `payload.id` and `X-Zernio-Event-Id` as the same stable ID, unchanged across retries/redelivery. It is an event identifier only, never an account/resource identifier. Source: <https://docs.zernio.com/webhooks/ads>; <https://docs.zernio.com/webhooks>.
- Delivery is at least once. A `2xx` returned within five seconds acknowledges delivery; timeout, non-2xx, or connection error are retried up to seven total deliveries using documented exponential backoff (~51 hours cumulative). Failed events then enter Zernio's dead-letter queue. Under queue load Zernio can park and retry enqueuing an event for up to 24 hours, so late arrival is documented. A lost response or slow (>5s) response can produce a duplicate. Source: <https://docs.zernio.com/webhooks>.
- When an endpoint is configured with a secret, Zernio signs `X-Zernio-Signature` as lowercase-hex HMAC-SHA256 of the raw request body, keyed by that secret. The documentation says to read/verify the raw body before parsing and not process mismatches. `X-Late-Signature` is described as a legacy alias. Source: <https://docs.zernio.com/webhooks>.

## Connection, Page, and form facts

- Zernio's normal Facebook OAuth flow has a secondary Facebook Page selection after consent; a connection belongs to a Zernio `profile` and returns a connected-account ID. An OAuth callback also includes `request_id` and `stage`. Instagram with Facebook Login selects a Page linked to the Instagram account. Source: <https://docs.zernio.com/platforms/meta-ads/connect>.
- Meta instant forms are Page-scoped. Zernio documents using either a connected Facebook account or a `metaads` business-login connection with a selected Page; Instagram lead ads use the Page's form. Meta Ads business login can bind an explicit granted `pageId`; without it, a new connection selects the first granted Page, while a reconnect reuses the prior Page. The selected Page can later be changed without a second OAuth. Source: <https://docs.zernio.com/platforms/meta-ads/lead-forms>; <https://docs.zernio.com/platforms/meta-ads/connect>.
- The `lead.received` payload has `formId` plus account/profile context but no Page ID. Therefore, Page-specific routing cannot be inferred from an event alone; a server-side, versioned mapping must preserve connected profile/account, Page, and form provenance. Source: <https://docs.zernio.com/webhooks/ads>.
- The official webhook routing model supports a profile external ID and an account ID. Events belonging to a profile carry `profiles[]` with its ID and configured `externalId`; endpoint filters can use `profileIds` and/or `accountIds`. Zernio recommends profile filtering because an account ID can change when an account is reconnected, while the profile does not. Source: <https://docs.zernio.com/webhooks>.

## Subscription, health, errors, and reconciliation facts

- Zernio ingests Meta leads via the Page `leadgen` webhook. Ads must be enabled for the team; only then does it seek the Meta `leads_retrieval` permission. A Facebook account connected before ads was enabled must reconnect to obtain that permission. A Page can lose the `leadgen` subscription while other events continue, so no leads arrive even though the connection otherwise looks healthy. Zernio documents a subscription-status read and re-subscribe capability for that future reconciliation case. Source: <https://docs.zernio.com/webhooks/ads>.
- Zernio documents lead retrieval by form and a cross-form lead listing filterable by `formId` and `since`. That is a future server-side reconciliation capability only, not evidence of actual delivery or provider health. Source: <https://docs.zernio.com/platforms/meta-ads/lead-forms>.
- For a disconnected/expired provider account, Zernio documents `409` / `ads_connection_required`, directing consumers to stop scheduled retries/polling until reconnection and then re-read account IDs. Its account-health endpoint reports fields such as `status`, `tokenValid`, and `needsReconnect`; Zernio's multi-tenant guide pairs an `account.disconnected` webhook with periodic health checks to catch missed disconnects. Source: <https://docs.zernio.com/guides/error-handling>; <https://docs.zernio.com/accounts/get-all-accounts-health>.
- Error `code` and `type` are documented as stable while human `error` text is not; new codes/enums can be added. For `503 temporarily_unavailable`, Zernio says to honor `Retry-After` and check an uncertain timed-out write's outcome before resubmitting. It warns that not all creates are safe to replay; Meta lead-form creation is explicitly non-idempotent. Source: <https://docs.zernio.com/guides/error-handling>; <https://docs.zernio.com/platforms/meta-ads/lead-forms>.

## Documented scale and commercial facts

- The corpus contains an inconsistency: the Webhooks page says up to 50 endpoints per user, while Zernio's multi-tenant guide says endpoints are stored per team, up to 50. Treat 50 as a team-level planning ceiling pending provider confirmation; do not use one endpoint per HON school as an assumed scalable design. Sources: <https://docs.zernio.com/webhooks>; <https://docs.zernio.com/llms-full.txt>.
- The documented REST rate limit is shared across the whole Zernio team: 60 RPM for 0–2 connected accounts, 600 RPM for 3–2,000, and 1,200 RPM for 2,001+. Zernio advises reading response headers and honoring `Retry-After`, rather than hard-coding the table. Source: <https://docs.zernio.com/guides/error-handling> and corpus section `/guides/rate-limits` at <https://docs.zernio.com/llms-full.txt>.
- Zernio prices active connections across the whole team. A Facebook Page, its Instagram account, and a Meta Ads account count as three connected accounts; the first two connected accounts are free. Ads must be enabled for the team, and legacy/non-enabled teams can receive `ads_addon_required`. Sources: <https://docs.zernio.com/llms-full.txt>; <https://docs.zernio.com/webhooks/ads>.

## HON design implication anchored to the supplied readiness assessment

The existing readiness assessment says HON has no connection object, credential record, mapping, signature verifier, durable inbound event/dedup ledger, dead-letter/replay queue, reconciliation model, or integration-review queue. Before any future connector work, preserve raw-body verification; atomically record an immutable, school-scoped inbound envelope and event ID; resolve a current versioned profile/account/Page/form mapping; place the candidate into human Integration Review; then use normal CRM authority for any creation/attach decision. Do not infer communication consent, assignment, or a Page/form mapping from a lead payload. Source: supplied HON assessment `docs/assessments/PHASE_2_CRM_INTEGRATION_READINESS.md`.
